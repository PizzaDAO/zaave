# Zaave 🍕

A pizza-themed front end for Aave. Supply USDC to Aave v3 on Base and watch the interest turn into pizza.

- Your USDC goes straight into Aave's own pool from your wallet. You hold the aUSDC and Zaave never touches your funds.
- Pick your pie price, and the app shows your interest as pies and slices, plus how many pies a month your stake earns.
- There's no Zaave smart contract, no fee and no token.

Live at https://zaave.vercel.app. The app is in [`app/`](app/), and the plan is in [`plans/zaave.md`](plans/zaave.md).

An earlier design used a custom vault contract. It's preserved at the `custom-vault-contract` git tag.
